A slower way to make

Good things take a few careful steps.

From a bottle that has already had a life to a piece ready for the next one.

01

Collect & clean

Locally sourced bottles are soaked, de-labelled and sorted by colour and shape.

02

Cut & smooth

Glass is carefully measured and cut, then every edge is ground safe and smooth.

03

Shape & assemble

Individual pieces are brought together as pyramids, faceted forms and little frames.

04

Finish & send

Each piece is checked, polished and wrapped with care in protective packaging.

The little details

A few good questions.

Still wondering about something? We’re happy to help you find the right piece.

Is the glass safe to handle?

Yes. All cut edges are ground and polished smooth, and each piece is inspected before it leaves the workshop.

How much is delivery?

Nairobi delivery is KSh 300, and it’s on us for orders over KSh 5,000. Nationwide orders travel by courier or bus parcel; we’ll confirm the delivery charge before your order is finalised.

How do I pay?

We accept M-Pesa Paybill 542542 (account 98736), PayPal, Visa and Mastercard. Cash on delivery is also available within Nairobi. We’ll confirm your order and share payment instructions on WhatsApp.

Can I request a different size or colour?

Often, yes. Glass colours depend on the bottles available, so send us your idea and we’ll let you know what’s possible and how long it will take.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we’ll arrange a replacement.
